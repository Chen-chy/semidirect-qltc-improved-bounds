# References and version pins

These are external mathematical inputs, not bundled source code or formally verified library imports. The theorem and lemma numbers below are tied to the specified versions. Metadata was checked against the arXiv abstract pages when preparing this source snapshot; the substantive theorem-interface audit belongs to the earlier research record.

## R1 — Classical symmetric code family

Tali Kaufman and Alexander Lubotzky. *Edge Transitive Ramanujan Graphs and Highly Symmetric LDPC Good Codes*. **arXiv:1108.2960v1**, submitted 2011-08-15.

[Versioned record](https://arxiv.org/abs/1108.2960v1).
The manuscript uses Theorems 3, 4, 6 and 11 for the fixed-parameter group/code family, coordinate symmetry, and classical rate/distance input. Expansion of the extended symmetry group is separately calculated in the manuscript; it is not inferred from symmetry alone.

## R2 — Local product expansion

Gleb Kalachev and Pavel Panteleev. *Maximally Extendable Product Codes are Good Coboundary Expanders*. **arXiv:2501.01411v2**, revised 2025-10-21.

[Versioned record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.01411v2).
The manuscript uses Corollary 3 and Lemma E2 for simultaneous product expansion of a tuple and its all-dual tuple, and inheritance by subtuples. The order of authors here follows the versioned arXiv record. DLV supplies the subsequent local-robustness interface.

## R3 — Cubical transfer and corrected rate argument

Irit Dinur, Ting-Chun Lin and Thomas Vidick. *Expansion of higher-dimensional cubical complexes with application to quantum locally testable codes*. **arXiv:2402.07476v3**, revised 2025-09-04.

[Versioned record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.07476v3).
The manuscript uses Definition 6.4; Theorem 3.6 and equations (14), (15); Theorem 4.1 and Lemma 4.2; Definition 5.9, Theorem 5.10 and Lemma 5.13. This version explicitly corrects the rate analysis by shifting the complex. Earlier unshifted counting arguments must not be substituted for this input. The displayed conservative constants in the proof are derived substitutions, not a claim of optimized constants in the source.

## R4 — Semidirect expansion background

Noga Alon, Alexander Lubotzky and Avi Wigderson. *Semi-direct product in groups and zig-zag product in graphs: connections and applications*.

[Author-hosted manuscript](https://web.math.princeton.edu/~nalon/PDFS/alwproc1.pdf).
Background attribution only: the norm estimate needed here is written out in the proof. This item is not an additional black-box theorem on the critical dependency path.
